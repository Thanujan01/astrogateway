# Stage 04 original asset and rights ledger

All new meshes, textures, shader code, stencil glyphs and assemblies were procedurally authored for SK2LB-AstroLab with Codex during this task. They are original project work; no external asset pack, photograph, font, sound, NASA model or purchased content was imported. Unity primitive meshes and URP Lit are supplied by the installed Unity Editor/package and remain subject to the project’s Unity terms. No separate third-party asset license was assumed or granted.

Geology and palette are artistic interpretations informed by the approved research. Architecture is a fictional training-outpost proposal, not a NASA design or engineering certification. No NASA logos or endorsement marks were used.

Reference links (no source files copied):

- [NASA Jezero panorama, PIA24921](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/detailed-panorama-of-mars-jezero-crater-delta/): bedding, heterogeneous eroded rocks; main image enhanced color, Figure A natural color; NASA/JPL-Caltech/ASU/MSSS.
- [NASA Mars facts](https://science.nasa.gov/mars/facts/): muted heterogeneous dusty setting.
- [USGS Jezero floor study](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/aqueously-altered-igneous-rocks-sampled-floor-jezero-crater-mars): igneous comparison-site context.
- [NASA greenhouse prototype](https://www.nasa.gov/science-research/lunar-martian-greenhouses-designed-to-mimic-those-on-earth/): protected controlled-environment module concept, not a deployed Mars habitat.
- [NASA media guidance](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/): future media imports require item-specific checks; none performed/imported here.
