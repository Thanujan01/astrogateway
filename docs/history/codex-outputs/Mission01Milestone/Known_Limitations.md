# Known limitations

- Procedural Earth/rocket/trainee are obvious blockouts; no cinematic realism, rigged character, finished animations or geographic Earth fidelity claimed.
- Dark IMGUI UI is functional prototype UI. Mouse/button layout, accessibility, small-screen responsiveness, fades and audio need human QA.
- Local callsign is not a separate account or Supabase display-name update. No email/password/UUID/token handoff added.
- Continue is a local onboarding checkpoint only; no cloud sync, inventory, full mission progress or account-bound save.
- Space journey, rocket flight, Mars landing and actual scene connection are deferred per the requested first-milestone stop.
- Extended key bindings and survival HUD are deferred; Earth controls do not affect existing Mars controls.
- Automated Play Mode is actual Unity execution but synthetic input. Fresh main-Editor hands-on controls and Android tests not performed.
- QuickSearch indexing exceptions occur in validation logs; do not claim zero Console errors.
- No new performance measurements or packaged builds. Requested FPS caps are not FPS results.
- Earlier compile/harness failures are retained in logs; final checks passed after specific corrections.
